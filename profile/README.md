### `// about`

We started in 2021 with a single Minecraft server.
Five years, a studio, and two platforms later, we are taking everything we learned
from building for players and creators — and pointing it at the wider digital world.

From game assets to virtual avatars to the infrastructure underneath,
we build the pieces that let creators ship worlds.

---

### `// what we build`

| | Service | Status | |
| :-: | --- | :-: | --- |
| `01` | **LUCY STORE** | `LIVE` | A marketplace for Minecraft plugins, resource packs, and 3D models — built on years of running our own servers. |
| `02` | **LUCY CLOUD** | `LIVE` | Minecraft server hosting with reliable infrastructure and a management panel that gets out of your way. |
| `03` | **Game Dev Assets** | `SOON` | Production-ready 3D assets for Unity, Unreal Engine, and Blender. |
| `04` | **Metaverse Systems** | `SOON` | End-to-end solutions for virtual creators — VRChat avatars, VR full-body tracking, and VTuber models. |
